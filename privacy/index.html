Plain-language policy · Effective 27 August 2026

Privacy

Your game stays in your browser. We do not know who you are, how fast you play, or whether you finish.

What is stored

The game stores your progress and motion preference in your browser's local storage. A copied save link contains that same game state in its URL. It does not contain your name, email address, account identifier, or device details.

What is sent

Nothing is sent by the game itself. There are no analytics, cookies, accounts, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. The static hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as an IP address and user agent to deliver the files and protect the service.

Your choices

Use “Restart” to remove the current save, or clear site data in your browser to remove all local settings. Anyone who receives a save URL can read and resume the game state it contains, so share those links only when you intend to.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public project repository linked by the hosting organization. This free episode does not collect contact details.
